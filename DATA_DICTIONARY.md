# DURUM Urban Runoff Microplastic Database — Data Dictionary

This file provides a concise one-line description for every variable in the *Urban Runoff MP Database_with_CF.xlsx* dataset.

---

## **Column Descriptions**

**ID** — Unique study identifier assigned in the DURUM database.  
**Author (s) and Publication Year** — Primary authors and year of publication of the study.  
**DOI** — Digital Object Identifier for the referenced publication.  
**Sampling location** — Land-use type where samples were collected (e.g., residential, highway).  
**Land Use Tags** — Standardized land-use categories assigned for analysis.  
**Country** — Country where the sampling study was conducted.  
**Location** — City/town or general geographic locality of the sampling site.  
**Region** — Larger geographic region (continent, sub-region, or administrative zone).  
**Latitude** — Reported latitude coordinate of the sampling site.  
**Longitude** — Reported longitude coordinate of the sampling site.  
**Area** — Area of the site or drainage zone as reported in the literature.  
**Sampling method** — Microplastic sampling approach used in the study.  
**Sampling method (standardized)** — Harmonized sampling method category used for comparison.  
**Quantification method** — Technique used to identify and count microplastics.  
**Quantification method (standardized)** — Harmonized microplastic quantification category.  
**Date** — Sampling date or date range reported in the study.  
**Total rainfall** — Total precipitation during the event or sampling period.  
**Temperature** — Air temperature conditions during sampling.  
**Antecedent dry days** — Number of days without rainfall before the sampling event.  
**Average wind speed** — Mean wind speed during the sampling period.  
**Measured MPs** — Reported microplastic count or concentration from the study.  
**Unit** — Unit originally used by the authors to report concentration.  
**Sample volume** — Volume of water sampled as reported by the authors.  
**Sample volume (L)** — Sample volume converted to liters.  
**Sample volume bin** — Standardized category for sample volume range.  
**MP concentration** — Reported microplastic concentration from the literature.  
**Unit.1** — Unit associated with MP concentration (duplicate column in original data).  
**Standardized Concentration (particles/L)** — MP concentration converted to particles per liter.  
**Size range** — Size range of microplastics reported in the original study.  
**Controls** — Description of procedural, field, or laboratory blanks used in the study.  
**Identified polymer (s)** — List of polymer types identified in the study.  
**Dominant polymer (s)** — Most frequently occurring polymer type(s).  
**Dominant size range** — Reported dominant microplastic size range from the study.  
**Numeric Size (µm)** — Midpoint of the dominant size range expressed in micrometers.  
**Standardized Size Bin** — Assigned size-bin category based on standardized definitions for the dominant size range identified.  
**Dominant shape** — Most frequently observed microplastic shape (e.g., fiber, fragment).  
**Decimal Latitude** — Latitude converted to decimal degrees (cleaned field).  
**Decimal Longitude** — Longitude converted to decimal degrees (cleaned field).  
**x1M_um** — Lower size bound (µm) reported for the study’s microplastics.  
**x2M_um** — Upper size bound (µm) reported for the study’s microplastics.  
**CF_1_5000µm** — Correction factor used to scale concentration to the 1–5000 µm size domain.  
**Conc_1_5000µm (particles/L)** — Standardized MP concentration normalized to the 1–5000 µm size range.
